  Every AI tool you use starts each conversation knowing nothing about your business.
  The judgement you have accumulated - how you write, what good looks like, which
  clients matter and why - lives in your head, so every answer comes back generic. A
  second brain fixes the supply problem: a structured, maintained store of your own
  context that your AI tools can draw on, built in a day from your own material.

faqs:

    a: >-
      A one-day, in-person course in Oxford where business leaders build a second

  - q: How is this different from the Oxford Agentic Bootcamp?
    a: >-
      The Agentic Bootcamp is about building workflows - pipelines of AI steps that do
      a task. The Second Brain Bootcamp is about building context - the store of your
      own knowledge those tools draw on. They compound: a workflow gets better the
      moment it can read your second brain. Start with whichever ceiling you have hit
      first.

    a: >-
      No. If you use AI tools most days you have everything the day requires. The
      work is deciding what your context is and structuring it - thinking clearly
      about your own business, not writing code.

The limit on what AI can do for you is rarely the model. It is that the model has
never met your business. This day fixes that, with your own material, in a room of
people working on exactly the same problem.
