The Oxford Agentic Coding Bootcamp is a practical, hands-on day in Oxford for people
who understand the concepts but have not yet built and shipped something of their own.

Claude Code and Codex do the typing. The day teaches the workflow around them: writing
the brief the agent reads first and asking for a plan before it builds, the editor and
the terminal, version control as an undo button, deploying to the web, giving the
product a real database, and checking what the AI wrote with tests and a second model.

By five o'clock you have shipped two things you own outright: a personal website live
on the internet, and a working prototype that saves real data.
