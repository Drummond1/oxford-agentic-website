Nicolai works at the practical end of applied AI: model fine-tuning, agent
workflows, and the plumbing that connects them to real business data. His company,
Jenesys AI, builds a multimodal AI platform for accounting practices, which means
the patterns he teaches are the ones he ships.

His particular interest is bringing agentic ways of working to non-technical,
operator audiences - finance and accounting especially - which is exactly the room
this bootcamp is built for.
