Emine's work sits where most AI programmes stall. Building a workflow is one problem; getting a team, a department or a board to adopt it is a different one, and it is the one she specialises in.

She co-developed the Oxford Agentic Bootcamp, shaping the format so the day covers not just how to build an agentic workflow but how to bring it back into an organisation that has its own habits, controls and politics.
