Mike runs Coppice Labs, a product consultancy based in Oxford. His work is the half of
building software that no tool does for you: deciding what to fix, what to build next,
and whether the evidence behind that decision holds up.

That judgement is where the Coding Bootcamp starts. The day's first move is writing the
brief the agent reads before it builds anything, and choosing what is worth asking for is
the part that decides whether you leave with something worth keeping. Like the rest of the
day, his part is show, then do, with facilitators in the room rather than a lecture.
