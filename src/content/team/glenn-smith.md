Glenn has spent two decades putting AI into production in places where it has to work: FX market-making at UBS and Barclays, then advising HSBC on AI strategy. Along the way he has had two startup exits.

He now runs two AI companies built on proprietary memory systems, and at the Second Brain Bootcamp he covers choosing the right memory structure for the job - and how a memory system scales from one person to a team.
