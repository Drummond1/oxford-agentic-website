Adam is a founding CTO with exits behind him and a master's in computing from Cambridge. He advises companies large and small while still building hands-on.

He built a second brain memory system for teams, where humans and agents share one memory, and at the Second Brain Bootcamp he covers team and company memory: what changes when a second brain stops belonging to one person.
