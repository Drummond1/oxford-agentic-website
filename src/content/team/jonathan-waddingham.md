Jonathan spent years as a product leader before returning to independent consulting, and he brings the discipline that makes the rest of the day work. The most common reason an agentic workflow disappoints is not the tooling; it is a task that was never defined sharply enough in the first place.

He has presented at and been closely involved with Oxford's Practical AI meetup, and co-designed the bootcamp format. His session covers problem definition and use-case discovery: how to choose the right task before you build anything.
